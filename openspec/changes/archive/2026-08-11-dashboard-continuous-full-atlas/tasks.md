# Tasks: Continuous Full Neural Atlas

## Authoring contract

Every executable line follows the canonical T### form, contains exactly one literal repository-relative path, and ends with an observable verification outcome. Tasks are ordered test-first inside each story. Outcome SC-003 and SC-015 are evaluated during independent final verification and do not create artificial product implementation tasks.

## MVP scope

US1 is the first independently testable MVP: normal visible motion runs continuously without the 1,800 ms stop/reheat cadence, deterministic community anchors prevent long-term collapse, and Pause/reduced/hidden/failure states remain exactly still. Completion requires pure adapter/layout evidence plus mounted consecutive-frame, Pause/Resume, visibility, and renderer-loss assertions.
