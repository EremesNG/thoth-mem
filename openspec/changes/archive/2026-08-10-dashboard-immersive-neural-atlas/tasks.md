# Tasks: Immersive Neural Atlas

## Authoring contract

Every executable line follows the canonical T### form, contains exactly one literal repository-relative path, and ends with an observable verification outcome. Tasks are ordered test-first within each independently deliverable story. Outcome SC-017 is evaluated in final verification and does not create an artificial product implementation task.

## MVP scope

US1 is the MVP: a mounted root route presents one viewport-dominant atlas whose world geometry is independent of the host, whose fit preserves irregular aspect, and whose point/link styling eliminates the giant-bubble and faint-synapse failures. Completion requires the pure layout/adapter tests plus a real-browser 1440×900 spatial assertion and reviewed whole-atlas screenshot.
